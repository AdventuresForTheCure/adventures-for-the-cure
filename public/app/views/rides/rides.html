Westside Worlds (WsW)

This is an UNOFFICIAL RIDE. You ride at your own risk!

Where: Starting from Catonsville Bikes (parking in the back)
When: Start times vary by season; check the WsW WhatsApp group for current times.
Description: This is a fast ride in Catonsville area which incorporates lots of different types of hills in Ellicott City. We will use this group has announcements when ride will begin. This is a full drop ride so please bring a que sheet if you don’t know the roads. We will divide into an A and a B group. In general if you race at the Cat 1-3 level then the A group is for your. If you race at the Cat 4-5 level then try the B group if it is your first time and move to the A group when you want more of a challenge.
