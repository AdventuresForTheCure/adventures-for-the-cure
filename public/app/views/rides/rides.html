Westside Worlds (WsW) Ride

This is an UNOFFICIAL RIDE. You ride at your own risk!

Where: Starting from Catonsville Bikes (parking in the back)
When: Start times vary by season; check the WsW WhatsApp group for current times.
Description: This is a fast ride through the hills around Catonsville and Ellicott City. Start times change with the season; check the WsW WhatsApp group before each ride. This is a full-drop ride, so bring a cue sheet if you do not know the roads. Riders divide into A and B groups. Cat 1–3 racers generally ride in the A group; Cat 4–5 racers may prefer the B group on a first visit and can move to the A group when ready for a greater challenge.
