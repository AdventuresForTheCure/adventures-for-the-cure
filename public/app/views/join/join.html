Join Adventures For the Cure

How to Join

Read this page and then email us at afc-board-of-directors@googlegroups.com and tell us about yourself and why you want to be on our team!

Our Team Mission

We want to use our love of endurance athletics to make a difference in the world! Sport can be a very selfish endeavor; we want to take it and make it into something that is more than just training, racing, getting fit, and having fun. We want to keep all of the 'fun' and then add charity, giving back, and selflessness on top of that! To join our team, please email us at afc-board-of-directors@googlegroups.com.

Membership Requirements

  1. You must volunteer in at least one AFC charity event per year. If you can't do that, then any volunteer or charity work in your community can be a substitute. You can see a list of the events we are hosting this year on the Events page.
  2. You must always show good sportsmanship, especially when representing AFC.
  3. When possible, help promote our sponsors.
  4. When possible, wear the AFC jersey to all your races. We do 2–3 jersey orders per year. When we start an order, we will email all of our members.
  5. If you are a registered USAC athlete, then you need to designate Adventures For the Cure as your team.

AFC welcomes members who may also represent other teams as long as they meet AFC requirements #1 and #2.

Member Benefits

  1. You get access to the AFC Team Email list & Discord.
  2. You get a chance to race with a team and people with similar interests.
  3. You get access to team training rides/practices.
  4. You get numerous discounts/benefits from our sponsors.

Membership Termination

Termination of team membership may result due to unsportsmanlike conduct or conduct that is considered to be against the AFC Mission Statement as deemed by the AFC board of directors.
