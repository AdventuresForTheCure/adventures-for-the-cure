Fundraise Through Cycling and Adventure

Adventures For the Cure is a Maryland-based nonprofit team helping people turn endurance sports and other adventures into fundraising and awareness for charitable causes.

Donate To Our Charity

Since AFC started in 2006 we have raised hundreds of thousands of dollars to fight diabetes (through Camp Nejeda) and to help children with disabilities in Kenya (through Kupenda For The Children).
